Why Am I Contesting?

Active Representation

Every student deserves a representative who listens before speaking and acts before making promises.

Connecting Students

Some students miss updates or hesitate to ask questions. I want to bridge that gap for the Lucknow region.

University Intermediary

I want to improve communication between students and the Saranda House Council.

Actionable Feedback

I want every student's concern and suggestion to reach the right people.

Always Available

I want every student to know that help is always within reach.

03

My Vision for Lucknow Region

Connected Community: Build a welcoming student community where everyone feels connected regardless of location.

Peer Collaboration: Create a culture of collaboration instead of competition through peer learning.

Regular Meetups: Continue that momentum with more regional gatherings across the Lucknow region.

Respectful Dialogue: Promote respect, inclusion, and positive communication in every interaction.

Active Participation: Build a region where every student feels confident to ask questions and share ideas.

Core Pillars

  • Inclusive Community
  • Peer Collaboration
  • Consistent Communication
  • Active Presence
04

My Action Plan

Regional Meetups

Continue organizing offline meetups across the region, with sharper focus on tech events and sports meetups so students can meet and collaborate in person. Taking inspiration from meetups happening at IIT Madras and Delhi, I will reach out to fellow regions and approach big tech companies to host or co-host our next meetups.

Communication

Send regular updates for assignment deadlines, exams, notices, and university announcements, so nobody misses a deadline.

Student Support

Be available to guide students with academic and general queries, and connect them with the right resources whenever they need help.
